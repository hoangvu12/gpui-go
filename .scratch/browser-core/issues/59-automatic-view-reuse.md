## Answer

Landed on `core/59-automatic-view-reuse` (Go only; main merged twice — 57 at 4d40f0b, 60 at 0e4d8ec).

**Design** (`gpui/view_reuse.go`, the phases in `gpui/view.go`): every entity-backed `ViewOf`/`ManagedViewOf` child grows the opt-in `CachedView` into a default. A clean view with a complete reuse record requests ONE layout node at its recorded root-node style (the drawable's `LayoutNodeStyle`) and decides at prepaint against the pin's cache key (bounds + content mask + text style) — hit: `reusePrepaint`/`reusePaint` replay the whole subtree (the pin's ranges); miss: render + `LayoutAsRoot` at the computed bounds, exactly the pin's cached miss. The provable-style rule ("when in doubt, re-render"): both size axes definite or exactly 100%, a plain container display (block/flex/grid), no overflow:auto root (the scrollbar reservation rewrites the node style) — content-sized views, other fractions, inline/contents/fixed roots always render fresh (retained layout is the prerequisite, the research's row 3). A view whose style stops being provable drops its record.

**What invalidates** (gpui-fast's reusable_record, GF:fast/retained.rs:587-626, over the port's seams): entity notifications — the view itself, its ancestors (the pin's `viewPathReversed` marking) and, new, every entity its render ACCESSED (a per-window entity→views index maintained from an O(1) log-mark capture over the frame's tracked-entity log; the pin left model notifies to the observe-and-notify discipline, which a default-on cache cannot); the window refresh; the drag and the active inspector (candidates off); scroll-handle versions — `SetOffset`/`ScrollToBottom` bump a per-handle version so an unnotified programmatic scroll refuses the reuse (the pin's "clamps at its next prepaint"); globals — a `Global`/`TryGlobal` read during the frame is recorded per window and a `NotifyGlobal` refreshes every reading window (full bust, rare by nature); the scene table gate below. Hover and wheel scrolling already notify the painting view (interact.go/scroll_element.go listeners, replayed with the paint range); a running transition/animation keeps re-rendering through `RequestAnimationFrame`'s view notify — no freeze (test-pinned). An explicit `DrawWindowFrame` of a CLEAN window renders everything fresh (the pin's draw semantics for hand-drawn frames); the frame loop's dirty draws keep the reuse.

**Reused views keep their frame contributions** — the pin's replay carries the prepaint/paint ranges; the port's newer frame records ride extensions of the same index machinery (`view_cache.go`): the tooltip records' seen marks, the modal focus entries and the titlebar control areas joined `prepaintStateIndex`, and the click/contextmenu registrations and top-layer overlay registrations joined `paintIndex` (the frame-end reconciliations drop unregistered entries — this also fixes a latent gap where the explicit `CachedView` lost them). The map-based records (debug-selector bounds, anchors, invokers, auto-scrollbar facts, user-select registrations, a11y nodes/listeners) cannot ride indices: each view's record captures them around its fresh render and replays them at its prepaint/paint position — including the deferred draws' own records at the deferred round (`deferred.go`), whose entity reads join the owning view's dependency index.

**The a11y replay** (the ticket's core): the record captures the subtree's pending a11y nodes (pop order, children lists, focusable bindings, focus/active-descendant claims) plus the paint-time action listeners; the replay re-pushes them under the current build parent — identical node ids (so the UIA identity and node generations stand), specs, bounds, children and actions, and action dispatch still routes through the replayed listeners. Chromium's model is the incremental `AXTreeUpdate` (ui/accessibility/ax_tree_update.h:19-36 — an atomic change between synced states, untouched subtrees standing); gpui-fast replays a reused subtree's a11y log (GF:fast/a11y.rs:1-30). The dirty-propagation shape is Chromium's incremental layout (layout_object.h:1477-1481 `NeedsLayout`; SetChildNeedsLayout + MarkContainerChainForLayout, layout_object.h:4203-4211, layout_object.cc:1690-1715 — stop at an already-marked container, like the port's ancestor walk).

**The scene table-index gate** (the replay correctness bug, from the orchestrator): native `replay` re-inserts a range's gradient/coverage/filter-op-chain rows at NEW table indices (reference/native/src/scene.rs:2789 `replay` → `insert_gradient`/`insert_coverage`/`insert_filter_op_chain` — `stop_row`/`path_index`/`op_chain_index` assigned at insert) while the copied quads/shadows/boundaries/coverage rows keep the indices baked in the previous scene; `SceneKernel.pathCount` (57's clip-path coverage seam) did not count copied paths. The Go-side fix: the damage log's entries carry a table classification (damage.go), `SceneKernel.Replay` advances `pathCount` + the new gradient/coverage/chain counters by the copied rows (a later fresh coverage insert's `nextPathIndex` is right again — unit-pinned), and a view whose recorded paint range (or its deferred draws' ranges) inserts or references table rows never reuses — `viewReuseTableSafe` — with a kernel-level alignment check in `Replay` as defense (a shifted prefix refuses the copy). `TestViewReuseGradientTableStaysRight` fails without it (the panel's quad would reference the sibling's row — or the kernel defense panics); `TestSceneReplayCountsCopiedPaths` pins the counters.

**The opt-outs**: `GPUI_VIEW_REUSE=0` process-wide (gpui-fast's `GPUI_VIEW_RETENTION=0`, GF:fast/retained.rs:292), `WindowOptions.DisableViewReuse`/`SetWindowViewReuse(w, false)` per window, `WithoutViewReuse(view)` per placement.

**Tests** (`gpui/view_reuse_test.go`): render counts (sibling change → [1,1,2,1,1,1]; root change → the panels unchanged; model change → every reading panel; the forced clean draw re-renders); the a11y tree identity with and without reuse (the full snapshot — roles, labels, children, bounds, actions, focus — compared after activation, a partial update, a focus move, a reuse with focus inside a reused panel, plus action dispatch through a replayed listener); the differential vs the opt-out (scene, hitboxes, hit test, dispatch tree, cursor, focus, click delivery — the pin's cached/uncached differential shape); the scroll version, the global notify, the transition keep-going; the gradient/clip-path gate; the counters. `gpui/view_reuse_measure_test.go` is the measurement harness.

**Measurements** (`evidence/browser-core/ticket59-view-reuse.json`, `R59_MEASURE_OUT`, interleaved pairs): one test window of 192 fixed-cell panel views, the reuse side vs the per-view opt-out baseline, a partial update and a root re-render, each measured as the direct dirty frame draw and the end-to-end notify+flush: the direct draw means 16.6 vs 17.1ms (partial, +2.7%) and 17.3 vs 17.5ms (root, +1.1%); the end-to-end 14.5 vs 14.0 and 14.8 vs 14.6 (−3.8%/−1.4%); reuse hits 26810/53690, zero misses. The honest reading: at this fixture the frame is FFI+GC-bound and ~neutral on this heavily-shared machine — the reuse removes the 191 panels' renders, prepaints and paints, but the root still re-renders (the ancestor rule), the layout engine still runs, and the replay pays one cgo per range while the fresh path pays batched inserts. The mechanism's ceiling is the existing explicit-path bench on the same machine: `BenchmarkViewCache DivsRootNotify` cached 8.4ms/122 native calls vs plain 14.6ms/3094 (−42%) — the auto path reaches that only for provable-style roots, and the perf fixture's panes are auto-height (no reuse). The deep wins need the native follow-ups the research names: the batched/bulk replay, the dispatch bulk copy, retained layout.

**Gaps**: content-sized (auto) views never reuse (the recorded style can't reproduce their measure — retained layout is the prerequisite); the dependency capture is per whole render (a nested view's reads also dirty its ancestors — coarse but safe); the global invalidation is a per-window full bust; the splicing (gpui-fast's #2) is not ported — a dirty view re-renders its ancestors' trees; the deferred-draw dep capture attributes the round's reads to the owning view; the damage rule is unchanged (58's Answer — it reads whatever `reusePaint` replays; the reuse widens the undamaged coverage).

**Notes for 60**: the property tick never touches the reuse (verified by the merged suite); an animating view re-renders through its animation-frame notify, so its property drafts keep publishing and the tick keeps updating — but a view whose transition has SETTLED and whose element's drafts stopped will reuse (clean) on the next frame, which is correct (the final style is in the recorded paint). The one interaction to watch: a reused view's range must never carry property-node groups — `withElementTransformPaint` brackets the group at paint; a reused range replays the group's ops but 60's registry (`lastPropertyAnimations`) is frame-scoped, so a running transition's view never reuses (its notify cycle) and a settled one replays its last painted (settled) group — the gate on table entries does not cover effect groups, which are order-based (not index-based) and replay-safe.

**For the broad set**: `internal/buildcostspec` — no new `Styled` methods were added (`WithoutViewReuse`/`SetWindowViewReuse` are view/window APIs, not styles). The `gpui` broad run, the parity/spec packages, `gpui-go-base` (read-only, `-modfile` copy), both codemods and the base repo all pass; the known pre-existing failures (portfixture TestFx0004, cmd/tsxdev, authorspec's two-window counter) behave as documented.
