## Answer

- **Wheel latching** (one gesture = one scroller): at the gesture's first event the walk starts at the innermost scroller under the wheel and latches the first that can consume the delta in its direction (`cc/input/input_handler.cc:2341-2405` FindNodeToLatch, CanConsumeDelta `:2292-2329`); the latched scroller keeps every later event of the gesture — no chaining mid-gesture when it hits its end (`ScrollLatchedScroller`, `input_handler.cc:2195-2330`). The port: the per-window latch `windowInputState.wheelLatch` (`gpui/mouseevent.go:838-845`), the transaction open/close hooks in the wheel dispatch (`gpui/filedrop.go:467-487`), the claim/take decision (`gpui/wheel_scroll.go:137-172` wheelScrollLatchTake) and the div listener's consult with its can-consume check (`gpui/scroll_element.go:208-302`, `canConsumeWheelDelta`); the uniform list joins with its scroll state as the identity (`gpui/listelement.go:1186-1204`). The pre-fix behaviour (nested scrollers moving together) was red first (`gpui/wheel_scroll_test.go` TestWheelScrollLatching).
- **The latching transaction**: classic phaseless wheels form one gesture for 500ms after the last event (`kDefaultMouseWheelLatchingTransaction`, `content/browser/renderer_host/input/mouse_wheel_phase_handler.h:20`), broken by changed modifiers (`mouse_wheel_phase_handler.cc:203-208`) or a phase-Ended/Cancelled event (`mouse_wheel_event_manager.cc:46-54`). The deadline is checked lazily on the app's injectable clock (`App.Now` — TestApp's virtual clock, wall time in real apps), so tests advance `TestApp.AdvanceClock`; the slop-region break is subsumed by the port's position-gated listeners (a latch whose scroller did not see the event is cleared at the dispatch's end, `gpui/wheel_scroll.go:103-114`).
- **overscroll-behavior (auto/contain/none per axis)**: the `OverscrollBehavior` style (`gpui/style.go:593-662` the type, `:1018-1023` the fields) with the Styled builders `OverscrollContain/None/Auto` and the per-axis forms (`gpui/styled.go:266-352`) and the regenerated Tailwind classes `overscroll-auto/contain/none` + `overscroll-x-*`/`overscroll-y-*` (`gpui/class_generated.go:541-559`, `gpui/class.go`'s unsupported list narrowed, `gpui/script/types/gpui-script.d.ts` regenerated). Contain/none cut the chaining at the scroller that cannot consume — the wheel walk claims it without consuming (`FindNodeToLatch`'s CanPropagate arm, `input_handler.cc:2362-2378`, `cc/input/overscroll_behavior.h` PropagatesX/YScroll) and the keyboard walk stops there (`RecomputeScrollChain`, `core/input/scroll_manager.cc:166-213`, behind the stable RespectOverscrollBehaviorForScrollBubbling feature): `keyScrollContainer.blocksChaining` (`gpui/key_default_action.go:669-681`) and the wheel cut in the listener (`gpui/scroll_element.go:270-273`).
- **Keyboard scroll target**: the focused element's chain, else the mouse-press node's chain (the element under the last mousedown, `pressHitPaths`), else the root scroller — `LogicalScroll`'s node selection (`core/input/scroll_manager.cc:216-232`, `mouse_event_manager.cc` MousePressNode via `event_handler.cc:2474-2477`). The port resolves the press paths into the CURRENT tree through the dispatch nodes' new element identities (`gpui/key_dispatch.go:293-330` SetElementPath/nodeByPath, `gpui/div.go:2114`, the same GlobalElementID key the hitboxes' ownerPath carries), in `keyboardScrollStartPath` (`gpui/key_default_action.go:404-429`).
- **Harness**: the `parity.Wheel(x, y, deltaX, deltaY)` step (`internal/parity/fixture.go:103-111, 226-233` — the platform's sign convention, negative deltaY scrolls down) records through CDP `Input.dispatchMouseEvent` mouseWheel (`internal/parity/recorder.go:616-632` — each CDP wheel is its own complete gesture because the DevTools handler stamps every one `kPhaseBegan`, `input_handler.cc:490`) and replays a `ScrollWheelEvent` plus a zero-delta phase-Ended event that ends the port's gesture (`internal/parity/replay.go:308-338`); the `Wait` step's recorder half now also sleeps its milliseconds of real time (the page's animations are already paused and seeked) so the browser's 500ms wheel transaction ends deterministically (`internal/parity/recorder.go:570-583`). Documented in `docs/browser-parity-harness.md`.
- **Fixtures** (`internal/parity/fixtures/wheel`, records in `internal/parity/testdata/`, Chrome 155.0.8059.85): `wheel-latch` (one 240px gesture over the inner scroller: it takes the whole clamped delta and the outer stays at rest — the pre-ticket port moved both; after the 500ms wait a new gesture chains to the outer), `wheel-contain` (the same nesting with contain: the new gesture after the wait chains to nothing), `keyboard-scroll-target` (a focused row scrolls the left scroller, a plain click blurs and the arrows scroll the RIGHT scroller through the press node), `keyboard-contain` (the inner exhausted by a wheel gesture, the focused row's arrows scroll nothing — contain cuts the walk). One deliberately-wrong view: the contain fixture without the contain rule fails Tier A on every outer-dependent box (`fixtures/wheel/wheel_test.go`).
- **Collateral**: ticket 07's `scrollrange` discrimination view now also drops the end padding — the press-node keyboard scroll scrolls the fixture even when the click misses the focusable row, and the top-padding basis cancels at the end scroll while the end padding does not (`internal/parity/fixtures/scrollrange/scrollrange.go:434-460`).
- **Tests**: `gpui/wheel_scroll_test.go` (the nested-latch transcript, the exhausted-inner begin rule, the contain cut per axis, the modifiers break, the press-node keyboard target, the keyboard contain cut — the first two verified red against the pre-fix code) plus the four fixtures and the wrong-view test; `go vet` clean, `gofmt -l` clean, `go build ./...` and `go test -p 1 -count=1 ./gpui/ ./internal/parity/... ./internal/layoutspec/ ./base ./internal/dropspec ./examples/zterm ./internal/tsxgen/` all green.
- **Limitations**: a wheel listener that calls `PreventDefault` does not stop the scroll (the pin's listener-carries-the-scroll shape, unchanged from ticket 02); the direction-reversal break (`ShouldBreakLatchingDueToDirectionChange`) is not ported (it needs the first GSU's ack state); Base's own wheel masks (`base/scrollable_mask.go`) do not consult the latch; `overflow: hidden` scrollers do not cut the chain (Chromium's chain includes hidden axes — the port's listeners exist only for scroll/auto); the keyboard walk still starts at the root view's element with no press node (Chromium's root scroller is the LayoutView — the port has no document scroller, so the root div must itself be a scroller); `overscroll-behavior: chain` (Chromium 155's fourth keyword) is not modelled.
