# Scene renderer strategy: selection and boundary

Research date: 2026-10-04; revised after main review. Selection proposal for [Choose the scene renderer and GPU backend](../.scratch/gpui-core/issues/07-choose-scene-renderer.md); main owns the tracker, integration, and the canonical contract drafted in parallel. Pin `254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a`; target PC per the recorded inspection. No implementation, probe, benchmark, or toolchain action ran. Sources: pinned `gpui_windows` renderer/devices/atlas/vsync, `gpui_render` build.rs and shaders, `gpui_wgpu` surface import, the capture frame type, the workspace/lock manifests, and the local `evidence/core` scene/plan/ABI/platform snapshots. Every capability claim carries its source; unexecuted behavior is listed as gates. No unmeasured size or performance claims are made.

## 1. What the pin actually renders with on Windows

**The Windows platform constructs the D3D11 + DirectComposition renderer.** `crates/gpui_windows` has no wgpu dependency; its `DirectXRenderer` is built from `ID3D11Device`/`ID3D11DeviceContext`, `IDXGISwapChain1`, and DirectComposition ([gpui_windows Cargo.toml](https://github.com/gpui-ce/gpui-ce/blob/254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a/crates/gpui_windows/Cargo.toml), [directx_renderer.rs](https://github.com/gpui-ce/gpui-ce/blob/254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a/crates/gpui_windows/src/directx_renderer.rs)). Device selection is now verified: `DirectXDevices::new` enumerates adapters via `EnumAdapters` until one yields a device — requesting feature levels **11_1 or 11_0** with `D3D11_CREATE_DEVICE_BGRA_SUPPORT` (plus the debug layer when available, `CreateDXGIFactory2` for factory 6) and failing explicitly if no compatible adapter exists ([directx_devices.rs](https://github.com/gpui-ce/gpui-ce/blob/254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a/crates/gpui_windows/src/directx_devices.rs)). A separate wgpu renderer crate exists (`gpui_wgpu`; the workspace requests wgpu `29.0.3` and the lock resolves **wgpu 29.0.4**); on Windows it operates through wgpu-hal **D3D12** with D3D11 capture import. Its `custom-gpu`/`wgpu-surfaces` features gate public custom-GPU/wgpu-surface APIs and interop — that gating is about those APIs, not a statement that the wgpu backend itself only exists behind them ([gpui_wgpu Cargo.toml](https://github.com/gpui-ce/gpui-ce/blob/254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a/crates/gpui_wgpu/Cargo.toml), [Cargo.lock](https://github.com/gpui-ce/gpui-ce/blob/254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a/Cargo.lock)). The pinned Windows platform does not construct it.

**Primitive coverage (all pinned, from the render loop over `scene.render_commands()`).** Pipelines: shadows, quads (smoothed variants), paths (rasterized into an MSAA intermediate — 4×, "guaranteed to be supported by DirectX 11" — then composited as sprites), underlines, monochrome/subpixel/polychrome sprites batched per atlas texture, surfaces (per-draw uniforms, `SurfaceColorFormat`), and blur (downsample → separable gaussian at half resolution on ping/pong targets → composite, plus a smoothed composite variant). `BackdropFilters` composite from the current target with bounds, content mask, corner radii + smoothing, max blur radius, and opacity. Content-filter subtrees use isolated group targets indexed by isolation depth up to `MAX_FILTER_GROUP_DEPTH = 2`; deeper nests render inline ([scene plan](../evidence/core/crates__gpui__src__scene__plan.rs)). The offscreen `scene_color` target follows scene-plan requirements, including backdrop filtering and isolated content-filter groups; it is not limited to nonzero blur. Font rendering uses global uniforms carrying DirectWrite-derived gamma ratios, grayscale/subpixel enhanced contrast, and BGR subpixel order — matching the text ticket's rasterizer parameters. Instance buffers cap at 256 MiB; per-draw `Dx11DrawConstants` are rewritten before each instanced draw.

**Scene accumulation is richer than sorted arrays.** `Scene` records a **paint-operation log** (`StartLayer`/`EndLayer`/`Primitive`/`Surface` entries), inserts primitives into a spatial `BoundsTree<ScaledPixels>` whose **order floor** `raise_order_floor` raises (deferred overlays stay above prior content), maintains a `layer_stack: Vec<DrawOrder>`, keeps `surface_opacities` separate from primitives, and supports **`replay(range, prev_scene)`** of recorded operations against a previous scene ([scene.rs](../evidence/core/crates__gpui__src__scene.rs)). `finish()` then produces the `ScenePlan` with `RenderCommand`s — batches plus `BeginFilter`/`EndFilter` with isolated/inline targets; filter boundaries are resolved by the plan ([plan.rs](../evidence/core/crates__gpui__src__scene__plan.rs)). `SceneBufferLayout`/`SCENE_BUFFER_LAYOUTS` record CPU sizes and field offsets consumed by shader tooling, with `ShaderBool` asserted to 4 bytes ([scene abi](../evidence/core/crates__gpui__src__scene__abi.rs)).

**Shaders are compiled at build time to embedded DXBC.** Rust-authored shader modules in `gpui_render/src/shaders` produce WGSL; the Windows branch of `build.rs` lowers it through naga 29.0.4 (plus naga-old 24.0.0 lowering) to HLSL and invokes **`D3DCompile` with `vs_5_0`/`ps_5_0` targets**, embedding the resulting DXBC byte blobs (`<label>.vs_5_0.dxbc` / `.ps_5_0.dxbc`) consumed by the renderer's shader resources; non-Windows builds are type-check-only and report `NativeWindowsBuildRequired` at draw time; wgsl-rs (git rev `b2de16a1b81e67d76bc265c296184f882864badb`) validates the sources ([gpui_render build.rs](https://github.com/gpui-ce/gpui-ce/blob/254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a/crates/gpui_render/build.rs), [Cargo.toml](https://github.com/gpui-ce/gpui-ce/blob/254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a/crates/gpui_render/Cargo.toml)). No runtime shader compilation path exists in the fetched sources — and none is needed by a pure-Go renderer either, since precompiled bytecode is embeddable.

**Swap chains, composition, and surfaces.** Both swap chains are **BGRA8, `DXGI_USAGE_RENDER_TARGET_OUTPUT`, `BufferCount = 3` (triple buffer), `DXGI_SWAP_EFFECT_FLIP_SEQUENTIAL`**: the composition chain uses `CreateSwapChainForComposition` with `DXGI_SCALING_STRETCH` and **`DXGI_ALPHA_MODE_PREMULTIPLIED`** (DirectComposition wants premultiplied output; path rasterization premultiplies in-shader while scene geometry blends straight alpha); the HWND fallback uses `CreateSwapChainForHwnd` with `DXGI_SCALING_NONE`, **`DXGI_ALPHA_MODE_IGNORE`**, and `MakeWindowAssociation(DXGI_MWA_NO_ALT_ENTER)`. No HDR or general interop is promised. `PaintSurface` carries order, bounds, content mask, and a `SurfaceSource`; the importable variant is `WindowsCapture` (a frame type wrapping an `Arc<ID3D11Texture2D>` plus display time — the pinned local file is only that wrapper, so the capture-session producer itself is **not verified** here). The D3D11 renderer attempts a direct shader-resource view on its rendering device, cached by COM pointer and retained per scene; an unsupported source is a native `anyhow` bail ("unsupported surface source") — distinct from the wgpu path's `DrawError::ExternalSurface`. The wgpu path is genuinely cross-device: it creates an NT-handle shared D3D11 texture (`D3D11_RESOURCE_MISC_SHARED_NTHANDLE`, `D3D11_BIND_SHADER_RESOURCE`) on the capture's device with a shared `ID3D11Fence` and `ID3D11DeviceContext4`, opens it on the D3D12 device, and falls back to CPU upload on failure ([wgpu shared](https://github.com/gpui-ce/gpui-ce/blob/254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a/crates/gpui_wgpu/src/wgpu_renderer/surfaces/windows/shared.rs), [upload](https://github.com/gpui-ce/gpui-ce/blob/254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a/crates/gpui_wgpu/src/wgpu_renderer/surfaces/windows/upload.rs)). Same-device direct SRV and cross-device shared-handle import are **not** equivalent; no universal sharing capability is claimed.

**Present, pacing, loss.** `draw()` is synchronous: `render()` records commands on the immediate context, then `Present(0, 0)` returns an error on failure. Pacing lives in the platform, not the renderer: the `VSyncProvider` thread does `DwmFlush` (interval from `DwmGetCompositionTimingInfo` with a sleep fallback) and invalidates all windows each vsync ([vsync.rs](https://github.com/gpui-ce/gpui-ce/blob/254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a/crates/gpui_windows/src/vsync.rs)); the Windows platform ticket assigns this to the Go host. Device loss drops resources, `ClearState`/`Flush`es, recreates devices/resources/pipelines/composition and the atlas, and skips the next frame; loss propagates through a custom message and an atomic flag; resize failure sets the same flag. No fence or query completion path appears in the fetched renderer source.

## 2. Options

| Option | Verdict | Decisive cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Extract and bridge the pinned DirectX renderer as a native service** (selected) | Pinned pipelines, DXBC artifacts, atlas, composition, and loss handling; the seam already exists (HWND + devices + scene data + atlas trait) | New paint-command/scene ABI and window lifecycle wiring; maintainer Rust builds |
| wgpu (pinned `gpui_wgpu` or wgpu-native) + Go scene adapter | Different backend than the pinned Windows default (D3D12 vs D3D11), different composition/vsync/surface paths; capture import becomes cross-device shared-handle + fence with a CPU fallback — an observable behavior class change; wgpu-native adds another foreign native artifact with its own C API | Parity risk moves from ABI to backend equivalence |
| Pure-Go GPU renderer port | Re-author/port every pipeline, the atlas, COM device management, and loss handling; D3D11 COM access without cgo means hand-written vtable dispatch; **precompiled DXBC bytecode is embeddable, so runtime shader compilation is not required** | Largest porting and validation surface; no inspected precedent establishes equivalence |

## 3. Selected architecture and responsibility split (audited against the scene sources)

| Layer | Owner | Boundary detail |
|---|---|---|
| Semantic paint command accumulation (elements emit layer push/pop, primitives, surfaces with opacity, deferred-order markers) | Go | typed command records; no OS types |
| `Scene` insertion semantics — paint-op log, `BoundsTree` with order floor, layer stack, `replay`, separate `surface_opacities`, `finish`/plan | Native (finite non-app kernel; the explicitly permitted small pure-Rust scene/planner extraction) | no entities, elements, windows, or app state inside |
| GPU packing (`SCENE_BUFFER_LAYOUTS`), pipelines, embedded DXBC | Native | build-time artifacts, provenance recorded |
| Atlas (textures, etagere `BucketedAtlasAllocator` per Monochrome/Polychrome/Subpixel kind, insertion/removal) | Native | logical texture/tile ids crossing; native tile mapping |
| Surface import (`WindowsCapture` direct SRV; native bail for unsupported sources) | Native | COM-keyed view cache, per-frame retention |
| Present, device-loss recreation | Native | device generation exposed to Go |
| VSync pacing, HWND lease, resize/occlusion, shutdown ordering, text-service→atlas mediation | Go host | per the Windows platform contract |

The audit corrected an earlier draft assumption: because `Scene` insertion involves the spatial `BoundsTree`, order floor, layer stack, and `replay` — not merely appending to sorted arrays — Go-side primitive accumulation would duplicate subtle pinned semantics. The selected split therefore keeps Go at the **semantic paint-command** level and lets the native kernel run the pinned insert/replay/finish/plan logic. This aims to preserve the pinned behavior while avoiding a second implementation of the ordering algorithm; extraction and wire adaptation still require the differential oracle gate. Ordering remains data-driven (surfaces sort by paint order at `finish`; deferred draws raise the order floor), so command-level differential testing still covers it.

### ABI contract (finite and pointer-free)

| Record family | Key fields | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| frame submit | device generation, window generation, extent, scale, background appearance, command counts | generation match; counts match capacities |
| layer push/pop, order-floor raise | bounds/layer token | floor monotonic; layer pairing |
| quad/shadow/underline/path commands | bounds, content mask, colors/gradient/transform, radii + smoothing, flags | mask/bounds sanity; count bounds |
| sprite commands | **logical atlas texture/tile ids** (not raw pointers), bounds, opacity, mask | ids validated against the native tile registry; tile bounds within texture |
| surface command | bounds, mask, capture-frame handle (native-owned), opacity | handle kind check; unsupported → native error status |
| filter commands | bounds, mask, radii + smoothing, max blur radius, opacity | begin/end pairing; native planner assigns isolation/inline targets |
| atlas insert/remove | texture kind, size, raster bytes (copied once) | size/stride validation |

The pin's `AtlasTile` being `repr(C)` is convenient but **is not by itself a stable public ABI**: the crossing uses private fixed-width records with validated, generation-safe logical texture/tile ids mapped natively ([platform traits](../evidence/core/crates__gpui__src__platform.rs), [directx_atlas.rs](https://github.com/gpui-ce/gpui-ce/blob/254b5dbd47cbb5acbcc5bbdcbb322a339276c88a/crates/gpui_windows/src/directx_atlas.rs)). All records use explicit widths/tags/counts, `f32` as bits or fields, bools as `u32`, version/size headers, and bounds/index/id validation; primitive arrays copy into native-owned staging; callbacks are fixed trampolines with generation tokens (frame-ready, device-loss, terminal acknowledgment) — never per-object allocation; no Go pointer is stored natively. Rust panics are contained in unwind-enabled exports; Go trampolines recover and latch. Shader provenance is part of the artifact record: WGSL source hashes, naga/naga-old/wgsl-rs versions, and the embedded DXBC blobs — the DLL never compiles shaders at runtime.

### Submission, completion, shutdown

A frame submission is a **lease**. Because the pin's only signals are the synchronous `Present` return and device loss, the Go adaptation defines completion explicitly, following the documented D3D11 mechanisms: an **event query** (`ID3D11DeviceContext::End` after the last protected GPU use of the frame's resources, `GetData` returning `S_OK` with `TRUE` on the immediate context) is the baseline terminal signal ([End](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/d3d11/nf-d3d11-id3d11devicecontext-end), [GetData](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/d3d11/nf-d3d11-id3d11devicecontext-getdata)); polling without flushing requires **ensured submission** — an explicit [`Flush`](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/d3d11/nf-d3d11-id3d11devicecontext-flush) when quiescing — since an unflushed command stream may not have been submitted at all. **Device-generation invalidation is not a safe-abort acknowledgment**: releasing Go leases after loss requires query completion *or* native-confirmed cancellation of old-device work plus released bindings and import ownership; absent that proof, affected resources are quarantined until teardown. No success acknowledgment is derived from `Present`'s return value, `Flush`, or timeouts. Pacing (DwmFlush) remains the host's and is never a retirement signal. Teardown rejects new submissions, drains in-flight presents, and releases COM/composition objects on the owning apartment before HWND destruction.

## 4. Unexecuted gates and marked unknowns

The final read-only side-chat review of the canonical contract found one concrete integration trap: the pinned renderer's optional DXGI-device member is absent with DirectComposition disabled. The selected capture adapter now queries `IDXGIDevice` directly from its `ID3D11Device` with explicit error handling. Main also specified coalesced arrival notifications, native frame checkout/copy ownership, valid content bounds and multithread protection. These are bridge design choices awaiting execution, not verified capture behavior of the pinned producer.

**Ticket 08 (distribution):** DLL provenance (toolchain, naga/naga-old/wgsl-rs versions, WGSL hashes, embedded DXBC), packaging/loading policy, clean `CGO_ENABLED=0` consumer build and run, architecture targeting.

**Ticket 09 (conformance):** differential scene oracle (same paint commands through the pinned kernel → identical command/batch/boundary output, including layer/order-floor/replay semantics); visual/text-geometry checks for every primitive class (smoothed quads/shadows, MSAA path AA, mono/subpixel blends against the DirectWrite uniforms, backdrop and nested filters at depth ≤ 2 with inline fallback, surface opacity and repeated-frame caching); device-loss cycle (generation invalidation, skipped frame, atlas/pipeline recreation); resize/minimize/restore and DPI co-invalidation; present-failure and scene-too-large propagation; **completion semantics** (event-query retirement, explicit-Flush-before-poll, loss quarantine); shutdown draining with outstanding COM references; surface same-device/cross-device compatibility — the inspected native renderer attempts a direct SRV and the wgpu source implements a shared-handle route; actual producer/renderer compatibility and synchronization remain unverified.

**Unknowns stated precisely:** the Windows Graphics Capture session producer is not in the inspected snapshot (only the 51-line frame wrapper is pinned); `SurfaceSource` variants beyond `WindowsCapture`/`Unsupported` were not enumerated; atlas eviction triggers outside explicit removal were not traced; wgpu's D3D12 import equivalence is explicitly not claimed; and no driver behavior (RTX 5050 or otherwise) is assumed — the PC inventory is context, not validation. Nothing here has been executed; no parity is claimed as passed.

Main follow-up: the [capture producer adapter snapshot](../evidence/renderer/crates__gpui__src__platform__screen_capture__windows.rs) was saved after the correction pass. It configures BGRA8 free-threaded capture and clones the raw texture with dimensions/timestamp; this does not establish same-device compatibility or frame-pool ownership safety. The [canonical renderer contract](../docs/renderer-contract.md) therefore selects and labels a same-device producer/owned-copy bridge adaptation, with explicit frame retention through copy completion and mandatory execution gates. It also governs computed draw order, ABI validation and retirement where this report offers only a sketch.
